## C. Items already supplied or resolved

- **Author:** Triune Biblical University Global Extension, USA — set on the title page in letterspaced capitals and recorded in the PDF metadata as the document author.
- **Place:** New York, USA — set at the foot of the title page and as the preface signature.
- **Chapter and part titles** were retained from the manuscript's architecture; the five part titles ("Foundations and Measurement", "Assessing Dispositions and Abilities", "Assessing Clinical Presentations", "Development, Culture and Relational Systems", "Risk, Integration and Communication") were written by the editor to group the sixteen chapters already present.
- **Numbering** follows the manuscript's decimal scheme (CHAPTER 1, 1.1, 1.1.1) and is consistent across all sixteen chapters; the contents page is generated from the headings themselves, so it cannot drift from the text.
- **Terminology** was standardised: *assessment* and *testing* are distinguished throughout; *diagnosis* and *formulation* carry the glossary senses; *client* is used in preference to *patient* except where a medical setting is quoted.
- **Obvious slips** in the source (article errors such as "a employee", "a acute"; repeated stock sentences; duplicated reference blocks) were removed at rebuild, not patched line by line.
- **Figures and tables** were redrawn or reconstructed from the manuscript's schemas without altering any datum; every one is referred to in the text.
- **No citation, statistic, case or quotation has been invented.** Where the source made a claim without support, the claim was either retained in a form that does not assert more than the source did, or listed in `substantive_issues.md` as requiring support.
