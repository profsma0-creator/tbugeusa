## 3. Items closed editorially, for the record

- **Dedication, acknowledgements, foreword:** none existed in the manuscript. None has been invented, and none is required for a course textbook. If the institution later wants any of them, each is a single leaf and the pagination reflows automatically.
- **"Author's" approval:** the delivered text is an editorial rebuild of a degenerate source (see `substantive_issues.md`); approval is a governance step taken by the institution's academic lead and does not change the file. It is recorded there, not here.
- **Permissions:** the copyright page carries the attribution for DSM-5-TR (APA, 2022) and ICD-11 CDDR (WHO, 2024). No third-party figure, table or extended quotation is reproduced; all figures are drawn from the book's own schemas, and the case material is composite and declared as such.
- **Spelling:** British English throughout, with original spelling retained in the titles of works and in proper names (for example *World Health Organization*).
- **Edition:** "First edition" is set on the title and copyright pages.
