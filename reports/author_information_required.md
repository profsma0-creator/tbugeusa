## C. Items resolved editorially (no author input needed)

Recorded here so that the author can see what was decided without them:

- **Chapter and part titles** were retained from the manuscript's architecture; the part titles ("Foundations and Measurement", "Assessing Dispositions and Abilities", "Assessing Clinical Presentations", "Assessment Across the Lifespan, Relationships and Culture", "Integration, Risk and Communication") were written by the editor to group the sixteen chapters already present.
- **Numbering** follows the manuscript's decimal scheme (CHAPTER 1, 1.1, 1.1.1) and is consistent across all sixteen chapters; the contents page is generated from the headings themselves, so it cannot drift from the text.
- **Terminology** was standardised: *assessment* and *testing* are distinguished throughout, *diagnosis* and *formulation* are used in the senses defined in the glossary, *client* is used in preference to *patient* except where a medical setting is quoted.
- **Obvious slips** in the source (article errors such as "a employee", "a acute"; repeated stock sentences; duplicated reference blocks) were removed at rebuild, not patched line by line.
- **Figures and tables** were redrawn or reconstructed from the manuscript's schemas without altering any datum; every one is now referred to in the text.
- **No citation, statistic, case or quotation has been invented.** Where the source made a claim without support, the claim was either retained in a form that does not assert more than the source did, or marked in `substantive_issues.md` as requiring a citation from the author.
