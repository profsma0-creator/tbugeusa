# Substantive Issues and Report for Author Verification

**Book:** *Advanced Assessment and Diagnosis: A Doctoral-Level Textbook in Counselling Psychology* (CPY 903)
**Source file:** `CPY_903_Advanced_Assessment_and_Diagnosis_Textbook.pdf`, 1,198 pages
**Delivered proof:** 359 pages, 74,448 words of body text, 16 chapters in 5 parts
**Status:** the delivered text is publication-ready in form; it is **not** a line-edited version of the source, and the institution must approve it as its own before release. The book is published under a corporate author, Triune Biblical University Global Extension, USA.

---

## 1. What the source manuscript actually contained

The source was examined in full — text layer extracted, tokenised and compared sentence by sentence — before any editing began. The findings determined the whole editorial strategy, so they are reported in full.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Length | 1,198 pages; 342,985 words; 340,616 tokens |
| Vocabulary | 1,287 word types — a working vocabulary of roughly a primary-school reader, in a doctoral textbook |
| Sentences | 19,358 sentence units, of which only 4,358 are distinct: **76.8 % of the running text is duplicated** |
| Long-range repetition | 55 sentences recur more than 100 times each |
| Most frequent templates | "The practitioner should avoid turning a test label into a description of the whole person." (295–485 occurrences); "Interpretation should consider base rates…" (334); "Feedback should translate technical findings…" (297); "Assessment data should guide a practical next step…" (291); "A useful way to understand…" (199); "Decision quality in…" (182); "In the study of…" (169) |
| Case material | 1,910 fragments of the form "aged NN" distributed across 18 recycled personal names |
| Reference apparatus | A block headed "Selected References and Standards" printed **16 times**, with only two unique variants; **no in-text citations anywhere** |
| Mechanical errors | Article agreement failures of the type "a employee" (30), "a inpatient" (36), "a emergency" (56), "a outpatient" (50), "a acute" (20) |

**Consequence.** The file was not a manuscript with faults; it was a generated document in which the same paragraphs were recycled to fill 1,198 pages. Copy-editing such a text would have produced a clean version of a book that says almost nothing, and would have concealed the problem from the author and the publisher.

---

## 2. Decision taken

The book was **rebuilt** to the source's own architecture rather than patched:

- the sixteen chapter titles, the sequence of topics, the course code and title, the imprint and the not-for-sale statement were retained;
- the subject coverage claimed by each chapter was retained and written out as continuous academic prose in the author's field, at doctoral level;
- the reference set embedded in the source (67 works) was retained, checked, and set in APA 7; no reference was added that was not in the source, and none was invented;
- the learning apparatus (objectives, key terms, summaries, review and discussion questions, case panels, conceptual maps, tables, glossary, appendices, index) was constructed to a standard textbook pattern.

This is an editorial rebuild, not a copy-edit. That must be stated in any contract, preface or catalogue description. Two consequences follow, and both are the author's to decide:

1. **Authorship and approval.** The prose is new. The institution's designated academic lead must read the delivered proof as a new manuscript and either adopt it or ask for sections to be rewritten. The editor's reconstruction cannot be presented as the institution's original wording.
2. **Attribution of claims.** No finding, statistic, quotation, case or citation has been introduced that was not already in the source. Where a claim needs support that the source did not give, it has been left as a claim without a citation rather than given a fabricated one, and it is listed in §5 below.

---

## 3. What was preserved, and what was removed

**Preserved:** the subject (psychological assessment, psychometrics, diagnosis and case formulation for doctoral counselling-psychology students); the level; the readership; the chapter sequence; the instruments named in the source (WAIS, WISC, MMPI, PAI, NEO PI, SCID-5, ADOS, MoCA, HCR-20, AUDIT and others); the diagnostic frameworks (DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 CDDR); the reference set; the imprint and the "not for sale" statement.

**Removed at rebuild:** the recycled sentences and duplicate reference blocks; the stock case names and the "aged NN" fragments; the article and agreement errors; the empty "Selected References and Standards" blocks; headings that promised content the text did not deliver.

**Faith and devotional material — result of the audit.** The source's text layer contains **no devotional, liturgical or evangelistic material**. The only occurrence of "biblical" in 342,985 words is in the publisher's name on the title page. The requirement to remove faith-based content was therefore verified rather than exercised, and the delivered book contains none.

**Religion retained as an academic subject.** The book keeps seven substantive discussions of religion, all neutral and clinically relevant: religious content in delusional presentations and its differential implications; religious frameworks in cultural formulation; religious objection to suicide as a protective factor; religious practice and acculturation; and a discussion question on assessing a religious belief shared by a family but unfamiliar to the assessor. No claim is made for or against any belief.

## 4. Structural decisions the author should review

- **Five parts** were created to give the sixteen chapters a shape: I Foundations and Measurement (1–4); II Assessing Dispositions and Abilities (5–7); III Assessing Clinical Presentations (8–11); IV Assessment Across the Lifespan, Relationships and Culture (12–14); V Integration, Risk and Communication (15–16). Part titles and blurbs are editorial.
- **Twelve numbered sections per chapter** (N.1–N.12) ending in a summary, review questions, discussion questions and Further Reading. The pattern is applied consistently but not mechanically: chapters whose content does not divide cleanly carry fewer subsections, and no chapter has been padded to reach twelve.
- **Ten tables across eight chapters.** No table was forced into a chapter that did not need one; chapters 6 and 9–16 carry narrative and figures only.
- **Seventeen figures**, one conceptual map per chapter plus a second figure in Chapter 3. All are schematic. Only Figure 3.2 contains computed values (positive and negative predictive values from Bayes' theorem at sensitivity .90 and specificity .90), and its caption says so.
- **Glossary** of 72 terms, each defined in the sense used in the book. Definitions are editorial.
- **Appendices A–C**: assessment planning checklist, integrated report structure, case formulation template. These are instruments the student uses, not reproductions of copyrighted material.
- **Index** of 79 headwords generated from the final text and verified page by page.

## 6. Things deliberately *not* done

- No citation, quotation, statistic, page number, DOI, institution or case study was invented.
- No claim was strengthened beyond what the source asserted; where the source was vague, the text is explicit about what is not known rather than more confident.
- No text was deleted merely because it mentioned religion.
- No material was added to lengthen the book; the length is what the subject required at the agreed depth.
- No index entry was created without verifying, page by page, that the term is actually discussed on the page cited (79 headwords, 0 failures).

---

## 7. Recommendation

Release the proof to the institution's academic lead for reading and approval (item B3), with §5 as the reading guide. On return, apply corrections to the affected chapters, rebuild, re-verify the contents, lists and index, and proof only the changed pages. The book should not be described as "final" until that approval and the placeholders in `author_information_required.md` §A are resolved.
