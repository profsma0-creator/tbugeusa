## 3. Spine (for the cover wrap)

Spine width = number of leaves × paper caliper. With 180 leaves:

| Stock caliper (mm/leaf) | Typical stock | Spine width |
|---|---|---|
| 0.08 | lightweight/bible paper | 14.4 mm |
| 0.09 | 70 gsm book paper | 16.2 mm |
| 0.10 | 80 gsm cream/white book paper | 18.0 mm |
| 0.11 | 85–90 gsm book paper | 19.8 mm |
| 0.12 | 90–100 gsm book paper | 21.6 mm |

The printer must confirm the caliper of the chosen stock; the cover designer cannot finalise the wrap until that width is fixed. Spine text runs **head to tail** (title at the head), the Anglo-American convention: title *Advanced Assessment and Diagnosis*, then `CPY 903`, with the imprint at the tail; keep type at least 3 mm clear of both folds.

## 4. Colour of the cover

- Cover: **4/0 full colour**, printed on the cover stock (e.g. 300 gsm board) with matt or gloss lamination as the publisher prefers.
- The supplied artwork is an RGB JPEG. Convert to CMYK with the printer's standard profile before output.
- The artwork is predominantly very dark navy. Agree the black build (rich black or single black) and keep total ink within the printer's limit (commonly 300 %); large flat dark areas are where mismatched builds show.

## 5. Cover artwork — what the designer must supply

The current front-cover file **cannot go to press as it is**. It is 815 × 1086 px (3:4 proportion) which is about 136 dpi at trim width: acceptable on screen, below press resolution, and it does not match the 2:3 trim. At full panel size it would have to be cropped about 20 mm across the width — cutting into the instrument list at the left and the frameworks panel at the right — or letterboxed 26 mm vertically. Neither is acceptable.

Required from the designer, at **300 dpi or better, CMYK preferred**:

1. **Front cover panel** — 152.4 × 228.6 mm + 3 mm bleed on all sides = **158.4 × 234.6 mm** (≥ 1870 × 2770 px). The layout must be re-proportioned to the trim, not cropped from the 3:4 artwork.
2. **Back cover panel** — 158.4 × 234.6 mm including bleed. Keep the lower-right area clear for the barcode reserve: a plain white rectangle of 40 × 30 mm, at least 20 mm above the bottom trim and 15 mm inside the outer trim edge, so the barcode has its quiet zones.
3. **Spine panel** — width per §3 once the stock is confirmed, height 234.6 mm with bleed.
4. **Barcode** — an EAN-13 measures 37.29 × 25.93 mm including quiet zones. The volume identifier is the publisher's to assign; a not-for-sale institutional text may be issued without a barcode, in which case the reserve stays white or carries the imprint mark.

All type on the cover must sit at least 5 mm inside the trim (10 mm recommended at the fore-edge and tail).
