## EPILOGUE — WHY GOD LEFT IT TO THE YOUNG

### The Young Were Never Meant to Replace the Fathers

The final synthesis: God does not necessarily choose the young because the old have
failed, nor does He preserve the old merely because they are old. Scripture presents a
deeper architecture in which **fathers preserve, elders interpret, mentors prepare, sons
and daughters receive, and new generations carry divine purposes into territories their
predecessors could not enter.**

*Key Scriptures: Psalm 78:5–7; Joel 2:28; Malachi 4:5–6; 2 Timothy 2:2.*
