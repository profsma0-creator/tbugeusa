Everything to this point has been analysis. This part is construction. If the covenant between generations is not automatic, it must be built — and what is built is relationships before it is structures. Mentorship must become fatherhood, or it produces followers rather than sons. Houses of transmission — household, assembly, school, institution — must be deliberately maintained, because each has a rate of decay. Fathers must prepare successors by methods that Scripture actually demonstrates rather than by methods that merely feel orderly. The young must receive by honouring, testing, asking, learning and then stewarding. And there must be a table at which both generations speak, because a pulpit runs in one direction and a covenant does not.
