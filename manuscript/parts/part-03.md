The question of the title is asked here directly, and the answer is not sentimental. God does not choose the young because youth is a virtue, nor because age is a defect, nor because the future belongs to the energetic. He chooses them because some things can only be built by people who have not yet learned why they cannot be built, and because a purpose that requires a lifetime of labour must be given to someone who still has one. Six chapters follow the pattern through the Psalms, through Jeremiah's protest, through Josiah's discovery of a book his predecessors had lost, through Paul's charge to two young men, and finally through the most misquoted verse in the New Testament — *let no man despise thy youth* — which says something very different from what it is usually made to say.
