The pattern described in this book is not an inference from scattered texts. It is concentrated in Christ, who is at once the Son who received everything from the Father and the Master who spent three years preparing a generation to carry what He had given them. He wrote no book; He made men. He spoke of followers who would do greater works than His own, and the sentence has been misunderstood in both directions ever since. He promised the Spirit, and the promise was fulfilled at Pentecost, where the outpouring fell on sons and daughters and young men and old men in a single event with no generational boundary in it. And He left a church whose structure, in the apostolic letters, is deliberately described as a family rather than an institution. Six chapters trace how the Christ pattern governs everything that has come before.
