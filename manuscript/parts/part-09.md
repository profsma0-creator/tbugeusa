A book that has argued for youthful reception must argue with equal force against youthful presumption, and this part does so without qualification. Revelation is not self-authenticating because it is vivid; every claimed word must be examined by Scripture. Gift is not character, and the most alarming sentence in the Gospels is addressed to people who had exercised spiritual power. Knowledge that is preserved and never obeyed is a form of self-deception so effective that a man can study his own reflection and forget what he looks like. And on the other side, the veneration of elders can harden into a resistance to God that uses the language of fidelity to mask the fear of loss. The dangers are symmetrical; so is the discipline required to avoid them.
