The book closes where the prophetic hope has always been located: in the future. Joel promised that the Spirit would be poured out on sons and daughters, on young men and old men, on servants and handmaids; Peter, on the day of Pentecost, said the promise was being fulfilled in front of him. Malachi promised that Elijah would come and turn the hearts of the fathers to the children and the children to the fathers, and Christ identified that Elijah. Between those promises lies the whole work of generational renewal. Six chapters consider what a generation that knows God looks like, why genuine renewal is not the rejection of the past but its fulfilment, and why the God who has never lacked a witness will not begin to lack one now.
