What does it look like when a generation actually inherits? This part answers with six portraits. Joshua leads a people who were born in the wilderness into a land their parents refused to enter, and the book that bears his name closes with a claim so bold it could have been falsified: *there failed not ought of any good thing which the Lord had spoken*. The builders after the exile recover a worship, a text and a wall, in that order. Josiah, at twenty-six, finds a book that his grandfather had neglected and his father had not read, and it breaks him. The sons and daughters of Joel's prophecy prophesy. And the generation that finishes the work discovers that it entered into other men's labours, and rejoices rather than resenting it.
