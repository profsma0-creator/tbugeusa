Every book about the beauty of generational transmission must reckon with the fact that the Bible is full of generational collisions, and that some of them were God's doing. This part examines six of them without smoothing the edges. Saul's fear of David was not a misunderstanding that better communication could have resolved; it was the old order attempting to destroy the new, and it very nearly succeeded. Rehoboam's rejection of the elders lost ten tribes in an afternoon. Elijah's despair produced Elisha, which means that exhaustion in one generation can be the mechanism of another's emergence. And John Mark, who abandoned a mission and caused a quarrel between two apostles, is named by Paul twenty years later as *profitable to me for the ministry*. The collisions are real. So is the recovery.
