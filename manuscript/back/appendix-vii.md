### Questions for Fathers, Mentors and Elders

These are intended for personal examination, for use in an eldership, or for a study group. They are deliberately uncomfortable; a question that everyone can answer immediately is of little use.

#### On what is in your hands

1. If you were to die tonight, name in writing the three things you know that no one else knows. Who have you told them to?
2. What is the largest thing God has promised you that you will not live to see? Who is it for?
3. Which of your convictions have never been said aloud in front of the person who will have to carry them?
4. What are you currently protecting that ought to be given away?

#### On manner of speech

5. When you last taught a younger person, did you tell them what you *know* or what you have *been taught to say*? Is there a difference?
6. Have you ever told a younger person about a failure of your own? If not, why not?
7. In the last year, how many hours have you spent in conversation—not preaching, not teaching—with someone twenty years younger? 
8. Is there a question you are afraid a younger member of your congregation might ask you?

#### On succession

9. Have you named a successor? If not, is that because you do not know who, or because naming would cost you something?
10. What did David do in the last years of his reign that you have not done?
11. Are you building anything whose completion would require you to hand over authority you currently hold?
12. When you correct a younger leader, do you do it in private or in public, and why?

#### On resistance

13. Recall the last time you opposed a proposal from a younger person. Was your objection about principle or about control? How would you know the difference?
14. Is there a younger minister whose success you find it difficult to rejoice in? What does that reveal?
15. When did you last change your mind because of something a younger person said?
16. Moses prayed that all the Lord's people would prophesy (Numbers 11:29). Do you?

#### On your own household

17. What does your household know about what God has done for you, in specifics, with dates and places?
18. Have you prayed publicly, in front of the whole congregation or family, for the next generation by name?
19. If your children were asked what you value most, what would they say? Would it be true?
20. Which of your children or spiritual children is furthest from God, and what have you done about it this year?

#### On finishing

21. What would you want said at your funeral? Who is best placed to make sure it would be said?
22. Have you written down anything—for the record, not for publication—about what God has done in your lifetime?
23. What are you doing this week that only you can do? What should you be delegating?
24. If your work were reduced to one sentence handed to the next person, what would the sentence be?
