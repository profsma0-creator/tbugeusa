### About the Author

**Revd Prof Adeleke Daniel Durojaiye (PhD)** is a pastor, teacher and theologian. He has spent more than three decades in pastoral ministry, and has taught biblical theology and pastoral theology at seminary level, with a particular concern for the transmission of the faith between generations.

His work has centred on the household, the congregation and the training of younger ministers — the three sites, as this book argues, at which a testimony is either carried forward or lost. He has supervised and mentored a generation of pastors, and it is from that long experience of watching what happens when the older generation speaks and when it does not that this book has been written.

He lives and works in Ibadan, Nigeria.
