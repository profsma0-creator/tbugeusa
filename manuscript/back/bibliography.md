### Note on Sources

This book is a work of constructive biblical theology rather than a survey of secondary literature. Where a specific debt is owed — the progressive character of revelation, the covenant as a structure spanning generations, the church as *creatura verbi* — the work in question is named in the text or in the section above. Readers who wish to pursue any single argument further will find the works listed here the most useful starting points, and the Scripture Index a complete record of the passages on which the argument rests.
