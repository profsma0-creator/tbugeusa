The passages below are arranged by theme rather than by canonical order, so that a reader who wishes to trace a single thread through the whole canon may do so. References are to the Authorized Version.

#### The duty to transmit

Psalm 78:1–7 — the governing text: hearing, keeping, declaring, hoping, across three generations.
Deuteronomy 6:4–9 — the household as the first school, and instruction attached to the movements of an ordinary day.
Deuteronomy 4:9–10 — "teach them thy sons, and thy sons' sons."
Psalm 145:4 — "one generation shall praise thy works to another."
Joshua 4:6–7 — the memorial of stones, existing so that a child may ask and a father may answer.
Isaiah 38:19 — "the father to the children shall make known thy truth."
Joel 1:2–3 — the calamity told to children and to children's children.

#### The sin of silence

Judges 2:7–10 — the generation that arose which did not know.
Hosea 4:6 — "my people are destroyed for lack of knowledge."
Deuteronomy 32:46–47 — the word commanded to be set in the heart and taught to children.
Psalm 71:17–18 — the prayer of an old man for strength to declare.
Proverbs 29:18 — "where there is no vision, the people perish."

#### The unfinished assignment

Genesis 12:1–3; 15:13–16 — the promise given, and the long interval announced.
Genesis 26:1–5 — the oath confirmed to Isaac because Abraham obeyed.
Genesis 28:10–15 — the promise renewed at Bethel.
Genesis 50:24–25 — Joseph's oath about his bones.
1 Chronicles 22:5–19 — David forbidden to build, and preparing abundantly.
1 Chronicles 28:1–21; 29:1–5 — the pattern received "by the Spirit" and given to Solomon.
Deuteronomy 31:1–8, 23 — Moses' charge to Joshua and to Israel.
Hebrews 11:13, 39–40 — the saints who saw the promises afar off and received them not.

#### The paradox of divine choice

Psalm 8:2 — "out of the mouth of babes."
Matthew 21:15–16 — Christ's quotation of it.
1 Corinthians 1:26–29 — "not many wise, not many mighty."
1 Samuel 16:7 — "man looketh on the outward appearance."
Jeremiah 1:4–10 — "I am a child," and the divine overruling.

#### The transfer of responsibility

Numbers 27:15–23 — Joshua identified, commissioned, and given honour.
Joshua 1:1–9 — the fresh promise: "as I was with Moses, so I will be with thee."
1 Kings 2:1–12 — David's final charge to Solomon.
1 Kings 19:19–21 — the mantle cast on Elisha.
2 Kings 2:1–15 — the request for a double portion and the crossing of the Jordan.
2 Timothy 2:1–2 — five generations in one sentence.
2 Timothy 1:5, 13–14 — Lois, Eunice, Timothy; the form of sound words.

#### The timeline of revelation

Habakkuk 2:2–3 — write it; it will speak at the end.
Galatians 4:4 — "in the fulness of time."
Daniel 2:20–22 — "he changeth the times and the seasons."
Ecclesiastes 3:1–8 — a season for every purpose.
Genesis 8:22 — seedtime and harvest shall not cease.
Mark 4:26–29 — the earth brings forth fruit "of herself," through stages.
Deuteronomy 29:29 — the secret things and the revealed things.
Colossians 1:26–27 — "the mystery which hath been hid from ages."

#### Memorisation as a seal

Isaiah 59:21 — "my words which I have put in thy mouth, shall not depart ... from this time forth and for ever."
Psalm 100:5 — "his truth endureth to all generations."
Deuteronomy 7:9 — "keeping covenant and mercy ... to a thousand generations."
Psalm 105:8 — "he hath remembered his covenant for ever."
Malachi 4:5–6 — Elijah, and the turning of hearts.
Luke 1:16–17 — John, in the spirit and power of Elias.

#### The dangers

Proverbs 1:7; 12:15 — the fear of the Lord as the beginning of knowledge.
1 Peter 5:5 — "be subject one to another."
Matthew 15:1–9; Mark 7:6–13 — tradition that nullifies the word.
Isaiah 8:20 — "to the law and to the testimony."
Acts 17:10–11 — the Bereans searching daily.
1 Thessalonians 5:19–22 — quench not, despise not, prove all.
Matthew 7:21–23 — the many who prophesied and were never known.
Galatians 5:22–23 — the fruit that gift cannot fake.
James 1:22–25 — the forgetful hearer.
