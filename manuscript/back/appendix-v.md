A thematic index of the passages most directly concerned with the relations between generations.

#### On fathers

Genesis 18:19 — commanding the household after him.
Exodus 20:5–6 — iniquity to the third and fourth generation; mercy to thousands.
Deuteronomy 6:6–9 — teaching diligently, sitting, walking, lying, rising.
Psalm 103:13 — "like as a father pitieth his children."
Proverbs 13:22 — "a good man leaveth an inheritance."
Proverbs 17:6 — "the glory of children are their fathers."
Proverbs 20:7 — "the just man walketh in his integrity."
Proverbs 22:6 — "train up a child in the way he should go."
Malachi 4:5–6 — the heart of the fathers turned to the children.
Ephesians 6:4 — "provoke not your children to wrath."
Colossians 3:21 — "lest they be discouraged."
1 Thessalonians 2:11–12 — "as a father doth his children."
Hebrews 12:7–10 — chastening as the act of a father.

#### On sons

Genesis 50:24–25 — Joseph's oath about his bones.
Exodus 20:12 — "honour thy father and thy mother."
Proverbs 1:8; 4:1; 23:22 — the recurring instruction to hear the father's instruction.
Proverbs 10:1 — "a wise son maketh a glad father."
Malachi 4:6 — the heart of the children turned to the fathers.
Luke 2:49 — "I must be about my Father's business."
John 5:19–20 — the Son who does what he sees the Father do.
Ephesians 6:1–3 — "children, obey your parents in the Lord."

#### On elders

Leviticus 19:32 — "thou shalt rise up before the hoary head."
Numbers 11:16–17 — the seventy elders and the Spirit.
Job 32:7 — "days should speak, and multitude of years should teach wisdom."
Psalm 71:18 — "now also when I am old and greyheaded, O God, forsake me not."
Proverbs 16:31 — "the hoary head is a crown of glory."
Proverbs 20:29 — "the beauty of old men is the grey head."
Titus 2:2–3 — the conduct required of aged men and women.
1 Peter 5:1–5 — elders as examples, and submission of the younger.

#### On youth

1 Samuel 16:11 — the youngest, omitted from the gathering.
1 Samuel 17:33 — "thou art but a youth."
Psalm 8:2 — strength ordained out of the mouths of babes.
Psalm 119:9 — "wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way?"
Ecclesiastes 12:1 — "remember now thy Creator in the days of thy youth."
Jeremiah 1:6–7 — "say not, I am a child."
Lamentations 3:27 — "it is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth."
Daniel 1:8 — the resolve of a young man in a foreign court.
1 Timothy 4:12 — "let no man despise thy youth."
1 Timothy 5:1–2 — treating "the elder men as fathers."
2 Timothy 2:22 — "flee also youthful lusts."
Titus 2:4–6 — young women and young men instructed.
1 John 2:13–14 — young men who have overcome the wicked one.

#### On the generations together

Psalm 78:5–7 — the three-generation chain.
Psalm 145:4 — "one generation shall praise thy works to another."
Joel 2:28–29 — sons, daughters, young men, old men, servants, handmaids.
Acts 2:17–18 — Joel read at Pentecost.
1 John 2:12–14 — children, fathers, young men, all addressed in one letter.
