## Front Cover Description

**Title lockup (top to bottom):**

- `WHY` — white, serif, all caps, wide letter-spacing
- `GOD` — gold foil texture, dominant display size, all caps
- `LEFT IT TO THE` — dark navy, serif, all caps
- `YOUNG` — gold foil texture, display size, all caps (echoes `GOD`)

**Subtitle** (gold rule above and below, italic serif, three lines):
*The Mystics, the Fathers and the Hidden Architecture of Generational Revelation*

**Imagery:**

- **Left profile** — an aged, white-bearded father in a hooded mantle, looking right/down
- **Right profile** — a young man with dark curly hair, looking left/up toward the light
- **Centre bottom** — a small figure of a young person with a backpack, seen from behind,
  standing at the head of a paved path leading toward the horizon, flanked by classical
  temple colonnades on the left and a modern city skyline on the right
- **Upper left** — aged parchment covered in Greek script (1 John, 1 Peter text visible)
- **Upper right** — architectural blueprint drawing overlaid with a sacred-geometry circle
  (Flower of Life / merkabah-style rosette)
- **Background** — deep navy-to-gold gradient with a luminous sunrise at centre horizon

**Author line** (bottom band):
`Revd Prof Adeleke Daniel Durojaiye (PhD)`

### Visual Thesis

The composition encodes the book's central argument without words: the old man
(preservation, memory, parchment) and the young man (reception, movement, the path)
face one another across a light they both look toward — **three generations in one
frame**: greybeard, youth at the arm, and the child on the road. Antiquity (Temple,
Greek manuscripts) and modernity (skyline) sit on the same horizon, with the blueprint
behind it all representing the "hidden architecture."

## Placement & Print Notes

- **Aspect ratio is 3:4 (0.750).** Standard trade paperback is **6" × 9" (0.667)** and
  a common e-book/A-format is 0.75. As-is, the art maps cleanly to a **6" × 8"** trim,
  or to most e-book storefronts (Kindle recommends 1.6:1 height:width — that is 0.625,
  so a **6" × 9"** or **1,600 × 2,560 px** version would be needed for a KDP paperback).
- **Resolution.** 1086 × 1448 px gives ~181 DPI at 6" × 8" — adequate for screen, but
  **short of the 300 DPI** needed for print. A print master at **1,800 × 2,400 px**
  (6" × 8" at 300 DPI) or higher should be produced before paperback submission.
- **Safe area.** The gold rules and subtitle sit close to the title block; the author
  band at the bottom is inside the trim. If a bleed/spine version is made, keep text at
  least 0.25" from trim edges.
- **Spine and back cover** are not yet produced.
