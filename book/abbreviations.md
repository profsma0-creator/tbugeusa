## Typographic conventions

:::table|
Convention | Use in this book
--
*Italic* | Titles of works; emphasis; names of statistical tests
**Bold** | Headings and, within tables, the labels of rows requiring emphasis
En dash (–) | Ranges of numbers and pages; interruption of sense

[…] | Editorial notes; square brackets mark information that the publisher or author must supply
%%
% | Used only in numeric expressions (for example, 16 per cent is written out)

## Notes on usage

Titles of diagnostic classifications are given in full at first mention in each chapter, with the abbreviation thereafter. The names of instruments are given in full at first mention, with the abbreviation thereafter; instruments are not abbreviated when the abbreviation would be unfamiliar to a general reader.

British spelling is used throughout, except in the titles of published instruments, organisations and works.
