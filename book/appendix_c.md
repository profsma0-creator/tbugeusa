# APPENDIX C|Case Formulation Template|Appendix C: Case Formulation Template

The template supports the formulation described in Chapters 1 and 16. It is intended to be completed in note form and then written as prose.

## C.1|The presenting pattern

Describe the difficulty in behavioural terms, with onset, course, frequency and severity. State the functional impact in the domains that matter for the decision.

## C.2|Predisposing factors

- Constitutional and developmental factors, including neurodevelopmental history, temperament and medical history.
- Family and relational history, including attachment experiences and significant losses.
- Cultural and social context, including migration, discrimination, material circumstances and community.
- Earlier episodes of difficulty and their resolution.

## C.3|Precipitating factors

The events or changes temporally associated with onset. Distinguish events from the person's appraisal of them, since the appraisal is often the more proximate cause.

## C.4|Maintaining factors

Identify the processes that keep the difficulty going:

- cognitive: appraisals, beliefs, attentional biases, rumination and worry;
- behavioural: avoidance, safety behaviours, reassurance-seeking, withdrawal;
- emotional: affect regulation strategies and their consequences;
- physiological: sleep, substances, pain, medication;
- relational: patterns of interaction that elicit and confirm expectations;
- systemic: family, organisational or service factors;
- social: housing, employment, finances, caring responsibilities.

## C.5|Protective factors

Reasons for living, relationships, capacities, values, engagement, future orientation and community resources. State how each will be mobilised.

## C.6|The formulation as narrative

Write the account in prose: why this person, at this time, in this context, developed this pattern; how the pattern is maintained; and what it costs and achieves. Use language the client could read.

## C.7|Predictions and tests

State what the formulation predicts: which intervention should produce which change, and what observation would indicate that the formulation is wrong.

## C.8|Intervention implications

Targets, sequence, methods and the arrangements for monitoring. Identify the point of maximum leverage and the barriers to reaching it.
