# PREFACE|Preface

This book is written for doctoral students and practising psychologists who are learning to conduct psychological assessment at a level that can withstand scrutiny — by a supervisor, a multidisciplinary team, a tribunal, or a client who reads the report and asks, reasonably, how the conclusion was reached.

Its premise is that advanced assessment is distinguished less by the instruments used than by the quality of the reasoning that surrounds them. Any competent practitioner can administer a battery. What distinguishes advanced practice is the capacity to formulate a question that matters, to select methods proportionate to that question, to weigh evidence of uneven quality, to hold several explanations in view at once, and to communicate a conclusion that is both decisive enough to guide action and honest about its margins.

Three commitments organise the book.

**Assessment exists to answer a question.** Instruments, interviews and records are means; the decision the assessment was commissioned to inform is the end that gives them sense. Each chapter therefore begins from the question and works towards the method, rather than the reverse.

**Every conclusion is probabilistic.** Scores carry error, informants disagree, and diagnostic categories are useful simplifications of heterogeneous presentations. The book treats measurement error, base rates and diagnostic uncertainty not as technical footnotes but as the substance of responsible interpretation.

**Assessment is an ethical act.** Its conclusions can open or close access to treatment, education, employment, custody and liberty. The practitioner therefore owes the client a transparent account of what was measured, what was inferred and what remains uncertain.

## Organisation

The sixteen chapters are grouped into five parts. Part I establishes the foundations: the purposes of assessment, the design of interviews, and the psychometric basis of score interpretation. Part II addresses the assessment of dispositions and abilities — personality, cognitive function, and achievement, aptitude and career. Part III takes up the major clinical presentations: mood, anxiety, obsessive-compulsive, trauma-related, bipolar, psychotic, substance-related and enduring personality difficulties. Part IV considers children and adolescents, couples and families, and the cultural, linguistic and disability considerations that condition every interpretation. Part V addresses risk and safeguarding, and closes with the integration of evidence into formulation, reports and feedback.

Each chapter follows a consistent structure: learning objectives; key terms; an introduction that places the topic in the broader argument of the book; numbered sections that move from principle to application; an Advanced Case Conference that demonstrates the reasoning on a composite case; a summary; review and discussion questions; and a reading list. The case conferences are fictional composites constructed to illustrate assessment reasoning, not descriptions of identifiable clients.

## A note on scope and limits

The book does not reproduce proprietary test items, scoring materials or copyrighted diagnostic criteria. Students must consult the authorised manuals of individual instruments and the current editions of the diagnostic classifications before administering or interpreting any measure. A textbook can teach principles; it cannot replace test-specific training, supervised practice or professional competence.

Diagnostic systems are revised. This edition refers to the *Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders*, fifth edition, text revision (DSM-5-TR), together with approved DSM-5-TR updates available at the time of writing; to the World Health Organization's *Clinical Descriptions and Diagnostic Requirements for ICD-11 Mental, Behavioural and Neurodevelopmental Disorders* (2024); and to the 2014 *Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing*. Practitioners remain responsible for checking later revisions and jurisdiction-specific requirements.

British spelling has been used throughout, except in the titles of published instruments, organisations and works, which retain their original spelling.
