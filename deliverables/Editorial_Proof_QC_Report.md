## Source-fidelity method and limits

The rebuild extracts the source using font, type-size, vertical-position, and paragraph-indentation cues. It validates the following source heading sequence in every chapter: Chapter Overview; Orientation and Scope; Phenomenology and Clinical Presentation; Historical Development of the Concept; DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 Perspectives; Dimensional and Transdiagnostic Thinking; Development and Course; Genetic and Neurobiological Contributions; Learning, Cognition, Emotion, and Behaviour; Relationships, Family, and Social Context; Culture, Language, Sex, Gender, and Identity; Assessment Strategy; Differential Diagnosis; Comorbidity and Diagnostic Overlap; Functional Impact and Quality of Life; Risk, Safety, and Protective Factors; Case Formulation; Treatment and Interdisciplinary Care; Real-Life Applications; Ethics, Stigma, and Professional Communication; Current Debates and Research Directions; Integrative Case Conference; **Chapter Synthesis**; Applied Case Workshop; and **Chapter Summary**.

The first-pass deletions were disabled for this rebuild. No repetitive-sentence filter, no “Chapter Synthesis” skip, no replacement summary, and no minimum-paragraph-length exclusion was applied to the extracted chapter sections.

### Transformations still present

“Source-preserving” here means preservation of the source’s chapter body and structure, not a facsimile. The following transformations remain and should be author-approved:

1. **PDF reconstruction:** wrapped lines are joined, end-of-line hyphenation is rejoined where the next extracted line begins lower-case, and paragraph boundaries are inferred from indentation. Exact word-count agreement is strong evidence against missing chapter-body paragraphs, but it is not a character-by-character collation.
2. **Mechanical language normalisation:** British-English spelling mappings, DSM/ICD hyphenation, a small number of grammar substitutions, and initial-letter capitalisation after extraction are applied. Official names are protected. This is not yet a complete line-by-line academic edit.
3. **Learning objectives:** each chapter’s source prose objectives are formatted as eight bullets; the final source connector is normalised so the bullet reads “Connect …” rather than “And connect …”.
4. **New navigational matter:** a detailed generated Contents, a List of Figures, running headers, proof page numbers, PDF bookmarks, the sentence “A visual overview of this chapter appears in Figure N.” (16 occurrences), the List of Figures redrawing note, and the cover proof stamp are production additions.
5. **Front matter:** the source’s four Publication and Academic Use Notice paragraphs and four Preface paragraphs are retained and typeset on proof PDF pp. 3–4. The source’s chapter-only static contents on source PDF pp. 3–4 is replaced by the generated detailed Contents.
6. **Production matter not reproduced:** source running headers/footers, the 16 per-chapter “Verified chapter body word count” notes, and the final “Textbook Completion Statement” are not printed in the book proof. Their counts were captured for this audit. Author decision #10 asks whether those production statements should remain omitted, be restored, or be moved to an appendix.
7. **Figures:** the 16 diagrams are redrawn rather than embedded as the original PNG images. Their placement and captions were checked, but label-by-label semantic reconciliation and rights remain open. Figure 16’s longest differential label was shortened to fit its box and needs author approval.
8. **Supplied front cover:** the author-supplied 1086×1448 JPEG (3:4) is stored at `deliverables/assets/CPY_902_Advanced_Psychopathology_Front_Cover.jpeg` and inserted as PDF p. 1 and the first DOCX page. It was fitted to the 7 × 10 in trim by extending the dark top and bottom edge rows rather than cropping side content. The artwork itself includes “TRIUNE BIBLICAL UNIVERSITY GLOBAL EXTENSION, USA — Prof. Adeleke Daniel Durojaiye (PhD), President/Chancellor”; that cover line was left unchanged and does not alter the corporate credited author on the interior title page or in metadata. At 7 in wide, the supplied cover is about 155 ppi, so a higher-resolution print source may be required.
