## Scope and limitations

This is a **working figure-insertion revision, not a fully edited or re-typeset textbook**. Existing production briefs around the figures are retained for traceability at this stage; some directions (for example, requests for biological feedback arrows) are not fulfilled where they lack sufficient specific scientific support. The new plates explain their narrower, defensible scope. Production notes and placement instructions must be removed or replaced with final captions during substantive editing and reflow.

The inserted topic maps do not replace any necessary scientific illustrations of anatomy, cellular mechanisms, or empirical findings. Such figures require chapter-specific evidence review and subject-author approval. Existing charts, diagrams, tables, and other embedded assets have not been certified accurate by this revision. No quantitative results, scientific pathways, external citations, or bibliographic details have been invented.

There has been no wholesale body-text rewrite, secularisation, glossary completion, citation verification, or final print pagination in this revision. The previous assessment describes those outstanding tasks; its authorship and missing-figure queries are superseded to the extent recorded here. Authorship is now resolved, and editorial creation of figures is authorised. Scientific approval of the new schematics remains outstanding.
