Each was checked for authors, year, journal, volume, issue where applicable, pages and DOI against a publisher, PubMed or PubMed Central record. Their content was not re-read for this chapter; the statements they support are standard textbook claims (glucocorticoid effects on hippocampus, extinction circuitry, reconsolidation boundary conditions, memory systems) that a specialist reviewer should still confirm.

## Known limitations

1. Two paywalled reviews (Arnsten; Craig) were checked at key-point or abstract level only. The chapter's claims about them are confined to what those sections state.
2. The chapter does not cite primary animal experiments directly; it relies on the reviews above. A specialist review should decide whether primary citations are required for a doctoral text.
3. No page numbers are given for paraphrased claims. Page-level citation would be required for a press-standard reference apparatus.
